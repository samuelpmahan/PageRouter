    What is counted

    One count per visit to the link. Nothing personal.

    • Each time someone opens the tracked link, thro saves exactly four things: the link code, the time (UTC), the website they came from (just its name, like www.youtube.com, or nothing if their browser did not say), and whether that was YouTube, another site, or direct.
    • thro does not store IP addresses, devices, browsers, cookies, accounts, or anything else that could identify a person.
    • Because no person is identified, one person opening the link three times counts as three clicks. Link checkers that only peek at the link (HEAD requests) are not counted.
    • Anyone with this page's address can see these numbers, so share it with whoever you are negotiating with.
