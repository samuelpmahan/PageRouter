## Wrong (must fix)

1. **Evidence panel contradicts its own footnote.** J-Milly: the legend row "Manufacturer guessed" shows **10**, the sentence under it says **6** timestamped rows were guessed. Bodanza: legend **20**, sentence **13**. The legend counts all rows including the 4 (J-Milly) and 7 (Bodanza) candidate rows that are also guessed. Two different numbers for the same word, side by side. Fix: classify evidence with the same precedence as `classify()` (candidate first), or label the legend "all rows, including candidate notes", or draw the bar over the 101 counted plus the excluded classes only. The same bar also mixes all 205 rows with a "Counted 28" sentence; pick one denominator.

2. **"3 further mold matches fall years outside the window (2018 and 2019 releases), so they are not release support" is wrong for J-Milly and leaks guessed rows.** J-Milly's three are ids 81, 82 and 89 (Cloud Breaker, Method, Method), all guessed-manufacturer, all matched to 2019-07-03 releases (offsets +2652, +2652, +2634 days). There is no 2018 release among them. The "(2018 and 2019 releases)" text is hard-coded in index.html. It also counts guessed rows (`guessedOld`) inside a sentence that is supposed to be about counted ones. Bodanza's "3" is DD3 (guessed, 2019) plus MD5 ids 173 and 190 (counted, 2018): a mix. Fix: derive the years from the data, report counted and guessed separately or drop guessed ones from the sentence entirely.

3. **The one release hit, "Discmania PD +50 days", overstates what the data shows.** Match is id 110: `talked`, "said aloud", confidence 0.7, one mention at 13:30 (810 s) in "I Brought Back a Retired Disc", a video whose other 18 Dynamic Discs rows are about J-Milly's own out-of-production Convict series. The matched release (`releases.json`, 2026-07-13) is "Horizon C-Line, Gavin Babcock 'Game Breaker' Signature Series 2026". The PD mold also has a 2008 original-release row that the page ignores (coarse date). Nothing says J-Milly meant the 2026 signature edition. The 22px headline "Discmania PD · +50 days" plus "Mentioned aloud in the video published 2026-09-01, 50 days after its 2026-07-13 release" reads as release support. The hedge ("edition not verified") is 12px grey below. Fix: either do not headline it (state "0 verified release matches; 1 mold-name match, edition unknown" and move the PD line to a lead row like the Buzz one), or keep it and put "mold name only; the 2008 original is the same mold name" in the sentence itself, not the footnote.

4. **Id 111 ("Dynamic Discsand Innova", mold "Felon; Firebird") is thrown away, though it names both brands explicitly.** It is a single row with two molds that lines up one to one with two brands. Splitting it would give Dynamic Discs 21 and Innova 2 (it is "quote from a friend", so the creator is not saying it; the alternative is to exclude it by that reason and say so). Currently it is excluded as "two brands in one row" by a regex (`/\band\b|Discsand/` plus `/and /`) that works only by accident (Trash Panda is safe only because `\band\b` is tried first). Fix: exclude explicitly by id with the real reason ("quoted from a friend"), or split it; remove the regex.

5. **Duplicate counted row.** ids 106 and 107 are the same video, brand, cleaned mold (Convict) and second (1118). Two different stamp variants, but after the mold-name cleanup they are indistinguishable on the page. 12 more counted pairs lie within 30 s of each other with the same mold. Headline "28 appearances" and "20 Dynamic Discs" are mention events, not distinct discs. Fix: say "mentions and sightings" in the legend/labels, or collapse same mold and same brand within a short window and show both numbers.

6. **Receipts are not all Capability Lab capabilities.** 48 of 236 receipts (`thro.ratio` x46, `thro.positionHistogram` x2) are capabilities defined inside `insights.mjs`, not in `vendor/capability-lab/catalog/statistics.json`. They call `statistics.sum` and `statistics.histogram` inside, but the receipt on screen says `thro.ratio`, and 40 percent-share/rate numbers (e.g. the "71%", "2.7" inputs, the bootstrapped per-video rates) trace to it. The brief says every number must come from a Capability Lab capability. Fix: either add these two to the vendored catalog (with examples) or compute the ratio via `statistics.sum` plus `statistics.mean` on a pre-divided array, and show the catalog id.

7. **Receipt inputs are not inspectable.** Hover "28": input is `[0,0,0,...]` a 60-long 0/1 indicator that the user cannot tie back to rows; the viewer sees "(302 chars)" truncation on phone; "+50 days" shows epoch days `{"x":[20732,...],"y":[18080,...]}`, not dates. Rule from Sam: if you cannot validate it visually, a user cannot either. Fix: next to the raw input, show a one-line plain statement ("rows 74-79 and 90-112 where counted = true") and for release offsets pass ISO dates in the label or show "2026-09-01 minus 2026-07-13".

8. **Abbreviations in text, against Sam's rule.** "vs" in "J-Milly vs Bodanza, appearances per 10 minutes", "Linked vs unlinked brands", "4.4 vs 0.5", "5 vs 5 videos" (write "versus"); "Q4" in column "Exposure minutes, Q4" (write "fourth quarter"); "t" and "Welch" are fine if "t" is spelled out once ("t statistic"). The brand-name tile "J-Milly" is a name, fine.

9. **"viewer-hours": absent (checked rendered text). Click rule: weak.** "Link clicks count taps on a link, not people, sales or revenue." exists, but only in a note below the table; the LINK CLICKS column header itself carries only "taps on a per-disc link the creator owns". On phone the column is not visible at all (see Design 7). Put "not people, sales or revenue" in the header small text.

## Weak (should fix)

The page shows "28 disc appearances in 5 videos" and "2 of 5" but never says it is one video, and the "2.7 per 10 minutes (0.2 to 6.6)" interval is the tell. Say it: "18 of 20 in one video".

11. **Six J-Milly rows are "Discmania Unreleased (name withheld)" (ids 74-79, confidence 0.5).** Shown as a disc in the Discs tab and as 6 of 7 Discmania appearances, in a page that goes to the creator. Publishing that a pro is talking about an unreleased disc can be a confidentiality problem for them and for Discmania. They also hold the whole "Hardest Course" video. Because the ceiling is not informative, it should not be the third hero tile. Replace with something that discriminates (share of runtime? videos containing the brand, "5 of 5 videos"), or put it small.

13. **Linked-brand row is near-tautological and mislabeled in spirit.** Bodanza "Brands linked in the descriptions +3.9 appearances per 10 minutes": Infinite Discs and Trash Panda are 65 of 73 counted rows, the "other" group is Kastaplast 6 plus Discmania 2. The "Lead, not counted: Discraft Buzz (manufacturer guessed)" line prints a guessed Discraft attribution naming a real person's video; labelled, so within the rule, but it is the only +day number on that panel and it is a guess. Consider dropping it.

15. **Welch row over-explains, in two places.** "A gap this size is well inside what five videos each can produce by chance; read it as no detectable difference." plus the heading "How sure are we" plus "small sample, intervals are wide" in the sub-header: three hedges for a row nobody asked for. The "disc appearances" tile and the pitch word "exposure" sit on mentions. "On camera" column is there (3, 3, 0) but small. Be explicit in the tile: "28 mentions and sightings, 6 on camera".

## Pitch

18. **The offer is below the fold and framed as emptiness.** On 1440x900 the amber offer section starts at y=930 on J-Milly (page height 1393) and 980 on Bodanza (1474), so the screenshot a creator sees first has no sell. The offer shows three columns of grey italic "after connect", twelve times, and a note "Nothing in them is measured yet." The headline reads like a spec ("Today: what was said and shown. With Studio: how long people stayed for it.") not a promise. Sam's tone: "If u sign up and connect Studio, this is the plan." Concretely: replace the three empty columns with one confident block, e.g. "Connect YouTube Studio. thro-go multiplies each of your 28 sightings by the audience still watching at that second, and gives you a per-brand exposure total for your sponsor deck: Dynamic Discs, 20 appearances today, minutes of attention after you connect." Keep one example row filled with the sentence of what the number will be, no fake numbers. Add one button-like line (what the creator does: connect, which permission, what happens next, what thro-go charges or "free during beta") since a pitch with no next action is a spec.

19. **Hedges that exceed the data:** "One mention is a lead, not a pattern." (fine once, but also the whole panel); "Mold name matched, edition not verified." (needed, but move into the sentence, see 3); "small sample, intervals are wide" under the creator name; "Describes the pattern; it does not say which came first."; "Reach is a ceiling... Ceilings overlap between rows, so do not add them." (the table headline already says "Reach ≤ (views)"; one clause enough). Hedges that are needed and should stay: "not viewers who saw it"; the clicks sentence; the banner.

20. **Over-claims:** none in numbers shown as measured. Possible over-claim in the offer note: "Studio retention plus a second pass over these videos closes that." States capability as fact; fine for a pitch, but "Start/end seconds for on-screen time are not in the appearance rows yet either" is an internal-data admission a creator does not need; rephrase as what thro-go does ("thro-go marks how long each disc is on screen").

21. **What J-Milly would care about and the page misses:** 12 Dynamic Discs Convict rows and 9 stamp and run descriptions ("J-Milly out-of-production series", "Robert stamp") show that Convict is effectively his signature disc; the page lists "Convict 12" only in the Discs tab.
