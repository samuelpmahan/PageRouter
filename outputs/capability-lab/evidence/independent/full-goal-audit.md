## Resource and trust boundaries

Modest rejection probes exercise runtime depth/call/value/trace budgets, statistics input/resampling work limits, watch action/byte/microscope limits, ML sample/iteration/work quotas, and explanation context/draft/state depth/node/byte/claim quotas. Cyclic/deep/oversized and tampered state inputs fail before being accepted as evidence. Browser receipts record configured Lab service plus verifier Node/Chromium RSS separately from Codex infrastructure; observed snapshots stay below2GiB. These snapshots do not measure lifetime peak and do not establish an OS hard cap. Bounded grade concurrency limits simultaneous test children; it is not an OS memory limit.

Source kind is caller-declared, and FNV identity hashes are integrity aids. Matching typed model evidence checks arithmetic against the retained artifact; it does not authenticate instruments or establish physical causation. Curated mechanism references support only their stated scope. Real browser pacing observations, illustrative geometry and synthetic configured watch faults remain distinct.

Historical failed receipts are preserved. The uploaded immutable Explain WIP reproduces its missing imports/schema failures; the later source repairs do not rewrite that candidate. The final accepted-source ZIP is a separate version, and its fresh extraction will record any delivered-tree failures before root closes the goal.
