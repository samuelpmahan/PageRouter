# Full-goal acceptance audit

This is an audit checklist, not a completion claim. Each line needs current executable evidence; missing evidence means incomplete.

## Foundations

- Statistics families: descriptive moments; order/quantiles/ranks; empirical distribution; robust summaries; weights and online updates; covariance/correlation; deterministic resampling/uncertainty; paired/group comparisons.
- Linear algebra families: vector/matrix arithmetic; norm/distance/projection; coordinate transforms; pivoted elimination/solve/rank/determinant/inverse; QR and Cholesky; least squares; symmetric eigen decomposition.
- Every registered operation has a clear input/output contract, standalone usefulness, independently checkable example and documented assumptions. Degenerate input, finite extremes, shape errors, property order and no mutation are checked.
- Genuine compositions reach multiple levels in each domain and cross the domain boundary; their traces demonstrate lower-level calls. Declared priority weights are displayed honestly.
- Browser interactions change input, execute, change output, display meaningful plots/matrices, and let the user inspect math and the dependency graph. Screenshot evidence accompanies actual interaction assertions.

## Watches

- Mechanical, quartz and software mechanisms operate from one declared elapsed logical time, with distinct tick meanings and exact boundary rules.
- Pause, selected-event step, reset, replay, rate changes and energy interruption have verified semantics. Rendering and explosion controls do not mutate mechanism time or event counters.
- Assembled/exploded views use the same actual state. Labels attach to components and read model variables; energy and timing paths have source-grounded explanations.
- Statistical timing observations and linear-algebra view transforms reuse tested foundation capabilities. Quartz counts use compact ranges with bounded microscope expansion, not an unlimited full-rate event log.
- Mechanism roles follow primary sources; exact-caliber geometry and unmeasured physical accuracy are not inferred from schematic animation.

## Machine learning

- At least one numerical predictor and a complementary learning task are fit with deterministic inputs/configuration; the workbench can train, inspect and evaluate them.
- Preprocessing is fit on training data only; complete runs/conditions are held out. Baselines, residuals, errors and scope of generalization are visible. Independent leakage probes must fail.
- Training/prediction genuinely reuse statistics and linear-algebra capabilities. Captured browser observations remain distinct from synthetic teaching faults.

## Explainability

- Explain a fitted model prediction with numerical contributions or other mathematically appropriate attribution; totals reconstruct the prediction and perturbations behave as claimed.
- Explain selected watch events with variables, causal paths, sources and counterexamples. Unsupported numerical or mechanism claims are rejected or explicitly limited.
- A prediction explanation, a model fact and a causal claim are distinguished in UI and evidence. Checked explanation outputs and independent tests are retained.

## Teams and delivery

- Exactly three requested Sol/Luna/Luna teams execute; the root uses only the reserved wildcard in addition. Orchestrators delegate product code and preserve owned file boundaries.
- Sols record delegation choices, observed outcomes and concrete adjustments. Evidence supports the improvements; no claim of changing model weights.
- Reproducible source, scripts, receipts and browser opening instructions are delivered. Resource bounds are exercised; final gate and independent review are current.

Only after all sections have authoritative evidence may the goal be marked complete.
