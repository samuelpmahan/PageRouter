About Us

We are Homeroom Heroes, a 501(c) registered charity dedicated to supporting educators and enriching classrooms across the nation.

Our Mission

At Homeroom Heroes, our mission is to serve as a pivotal bridge between educators and community supporters, fostering meaningful connections that enhance educational experiences. With a steadfast commitment to the transformative power of education, we empower teachers by providing a platform to articulate their needs and aspirations, while enabling compassionate individuals to directly contribute to the enrichment of classrooms nationwide. Through our collaborative efforts, we strive to cultivate a brighter future for generations to come, one classroom at a time.

How Do We Do That?

At Homeroom Heroes, we simplify the process for teachers to showcase their needs by creating profiles and hosting curated wishlists of essential classroom supplies through Amazon. Our platform enables community members to make a direct impact by selecting and sending items straight to teachers’ classrooms—no middlemen, no extra fees. Every donation directly supports students by enhancing their learning environments. Together, we bridge the gap between community generosity and classroom success.

Meet the Team

We are a passionate team dedicated to making a positive impact on education. Get to know the individuals behind the project.

Justin Brundage

Founder, CEO, & President

I'm Justin Brundage, Founder and CEO of Homeroom Heroes. My journey began in August 2023, where I was inspired by a local radio station's effort to support teachers by fulfilling their classroom wish lists. Learning that teachers face the brunt of an $850 supplies bill, drove me to find a solution.

Growing up in a close-knit community, I experienced firsthand the profound impact teachers have on shaping lives. That's what pushed me to start Homeroom Heroes: a direct bridge between people who want to help and the teachers who need it. Unlike traditional donor sites, there is no middleman, no red tape, just classrooms getting what they need.

When I'm not working on changing education, you can usually find me out exploring the Pacific Northwest with my family and cheering on my alma mater Wazzu.

Join me in honoring the dedication of our teachers and making a lasting impact on education.

I'm Samuel Mahan, a software engineer dedicated to driving technological innovation and making a meaningful impact in the industry. My journey in the tech world began with a passion for problem-solving and a relentless drive to learn and adapt. Today, I leverage my expertise to create robust backend services and firmware solutions, ensuring optimal performance and reliability. I'm committed to pushing boundaries and embracing new technologies to drive forward progress in the ever-evolving landscape of software engineering.

Tory Furr

COO & Secretary of the Board

Through my work as a property manager for income-restricted housing, I've witnessed the direct and profound impact that a good education has on a child's future. This experience ignited my passion for helping others, inspiring me to join Homeroom Heroes. My dedication to our community extends beyond my professional life, and in my free time, you can find me enjoying crafts, video games, hiking, and baking.
